Semi-formal attire. Garden-inspired colors are welcome, and comfortable shoes are encouraged for dancing.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation will indicate whether a plus-one has been included. Please enter the invited guest count when you RSVP.

We love your little ones, but unless specifically named on your invitation, our celebration will be adults-only.

Yes, weather permitting. An indoor backup space will be available if needed.
