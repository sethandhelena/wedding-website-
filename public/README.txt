To customize:
1. Change names, date, venue, story, registry links, and blog text in index.html.
2. Change the countdown date in the data-date attribute on the element with id="countdown".
3. Update colors at the top of styles.css under :root.
4. Replace the Unsplash image URLs with your own engagement/wedding photos.
5. The RSVP demo currently saves submissions to localStorage in the visitor's browser. For a real site, connect it to a backend, email service, Google Sheet, Formspree, WordPress, or another form handler.
